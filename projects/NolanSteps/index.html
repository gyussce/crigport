Programs

Ages We Welcome

Nurturing care, sensory play, and kindergarten prep for infants, toddlers, and preschool children.

Infants & Toddlers

6 wks – 2 yrs

Attentive care matching your child's natural nap and feeding routines. We focus heavily on tummy time, motor skills, sensory stimulation, and reading books aloud.

  • Nappies & bottle feeding tracking
  • Sensory play & basic signing
  • Tummy time & physical milestones

Preschoolers

2 – 5 yrs

A fun, play-based curriculum introducing letters, shapes, numbers, and basic science. We practice sharing, expressing emotions, and getting fully prepared for kindergarten.

  • Interactive arts & painting
  • Early literacy & math introduction
  • Social-emotional development

School-Age

5 – 12 yrs

Before and after-school care for busy working parents. We provide a calm environment to finish homework, enjoy healthy snacks, and spend plenty of play time outdoors.

  • Quiet homework support space
  • Nutritious afternoon snacks
  • Outdoor active recreation

Drop-In Care

As Available

Got a last-minute schedule shift, temporary drill, or medical appointment? Give us a call. If we have a vacancy in our group, we will take care of your child for the day.

  • Short-notice scheduling
  • Full sibling-group discounts
  • Includes all daily meals

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about enrollment, safety, meals, and scheduling.

We observe standard federal holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas). We provide parents with a full printed calendar of planned closures at the start of enrollment so you can easily plan backup arrangements.

Yes, absolutely. We provide a healthy breakfast, morning snack, lunch, and afternoon snack. All meals are prepared fresh daily and meet the USDA Food Program nutrition standards. For infants, parents provide formula/expressed breastmilk and baby food jars, and we handle all prep and sterilization.

Yes, we do. We accept Illinois Action for Children subsidies (CCR&R program) as well as military fee-assistance programs such as Child Care Aware of America (CCAoA) and the Military Child Care in Your Neighborhood (MCCYN) program. We will gladly help you fill out the application paperwork.

Yes, we are fully licensed by the Illinois Department of Children and Family Services (DCFS). Our home daycare undergoes routine safety and hygiene inspections, and all resident adults have passed rigorous criminal and background checks. I am fully certified in pediatric CPR, AED, First Aid, and food safety.
