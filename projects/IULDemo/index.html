Why families choose IUL

Three Things Your 401(k) Can't Do

Indexed Universal Life isn't a replacement for your retirement plan — it fills the gaps your employer plan leaves wide open.

◆

Tax-Free Retirement Income

Policy loans aren't taxable income. That means in retirement, you can pull cash without bumping into a higher tax bracket or triggering Social Security taxes.

▲

A Floor Under Your Money

When the S&P drops 30%, your cash value doesn't lose a dime. The 0% floor means you participate in gains but sit out the losses. Your worst year is a flat year.

●

Death Benefit Your Family Keeps

If something happens to you, your family gets a lump sum — income-tax-free. No probate, no waiting. The money is there when they need it most.

About

Marcus Cole

I got into this business after watching my parents lose a chunk of their retirement in 2008. They'd done everything right — saved consistently, stayed the course — and still ended up pushing back retirement by four years.

That experience stuck with me. I became a licensed broker in 2013 and have spent the last 12 years helping families build retirement strategies that don't depend on the market going up every year.
