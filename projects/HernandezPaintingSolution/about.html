Our Story

Proudly Serving Chicago For Over 10 Years

Perfect Place Remodeling Inc., led by Gilbert Rios, was founded with a simple mission: deliver honest, high-quality home improvement services to the Chicago community at fair prices. We are fully insured and bonded for your protection.

What started with a passion for quality craftsmanship has grown into a full-service remodeling company. Plumbing, electrical, carpentry, bathroom & kitchen remodeling, deck installation, painting, drywall, repairs, light fixtures, flooring — we do it all with the same care and attention to detail that built our reputation.

We're proud to be a business that treats every customer like family. When you hire Perfect Place, you're getting a dedicated team led by Gilbert Rios that takes pride in transforming your home into the perfect place.
